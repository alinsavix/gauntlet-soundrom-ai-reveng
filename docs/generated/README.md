# Generated Catalogs

`channel_engine_catalog.csv` is generated by `channel_engine_audit.py`. It
decomposes `$4651-$4B6A` into consumer-proven entries and meaningful internal
blocks, including assumptions, exits, clobbers, RAM effects, and next tests.
The generator validates 15 instruction-byte anchors before writing 14 rows.

`physical_output_catalog.csv` and `physical_output_table_catalog.csv` are
generated by `physical_output_audit.py`. They decompose `$4D02-$500C` and its
consumer-proven ROM lookups after validating 26 instruction-byte anchors.

`pokey_control_catalog.csv` is also generated by `physical_output_audit.py`.
It joins configured bytecode operands and pair-arbitration masks to the supplied
MAME POKEY AUDCTL definitions after validating two additional ROM anchors.

`bytecode_handler_catalog.csv`, `bytecode_support_format_catalog.csv`, and
`ym_voice_field_catalog.csv` are generated by `bytecode_consumer_audit.py`.
They resolve every opcode through the target-minus-one table and record
consumer-proven support-data grammars and all 42 YM instrument fields.

`bytecode_engine_catalog.csv` is also generated by
`bytecode_consumer_audit.py`. Its 26 instruction-aligned ranges provide
contiguous semantic ownership for the dispatcher and handler implementation.

`bytecode_callable_contract_catalog.csv` is also generated by
`bytecode_consumer_audit.py`. It gives standalone contracts for ten callable,
tail, and synthesized-dispatch entries nested inside those broader ranges.

`speech_lifecycle_catalog.csv` is generated by `speech_lifecycle_audit.py` and
records the IRQ-time TMS5220 state machine, metadata load, and atomic queue
transaction after validating 16 instruction-byte anchors.

`board_control_catalog.csv` and `board_control_transition_catalog.csv` are
generated by `board_control_audit.py`. They preserve the distinction between
Verified arithmetic/state transitions and Strong-inference debounce/pulse-
width interpretations.

`nmi_protocol_catalog.csv` and `nmi_entry_contract_catalog.csv` are generated
by `nmi_protocol_audit.py`. They record
the conditional boot-time indirect-write window, fast-init bypass, normal queue
mode, drop modes, every direct `$0213` writer, and standalone contracts for the
NMI vector and synthesized direct dispatcher after validating ten instruction
anchors.

`initialization_main_catalog.csv` is generated by
`initialization_main_audit.py`. It decomposes RESET, diagnostic,
synchronization, initialization, main-loop, helper, boot-handshake, and
RESET-vector-gate code while validating 16 instruction anchors and all three
ROM checksums.

`control_plane_catalog.csv` is generated by `control_plane_audit.py`. It covers
IRQ/BRK handling, atomic reset, chip initialization, the context pool, all
handler spans, and type-7 allocation through `$4650`, validating 27 anchors,
all 15 handler targets, and configured handler counts. The exact query/restore/
output-latch split brings the current checkpoint to 32 rows.

`support_staging_catalog.csv`, `fade_rate_catalog.csv`,
`volume_shape_catalog.csv`, and `ym_tl_bias_catalog.csv` are generated by
`support_staging_audit.py`. They close `$4B6B-$4D01` and record the exact
fade/ramp selectors, 8×16 POKEY shape table, and configured YM TL-bias indices.

`semantic_code_coverage_catalog.csv` is generated by
`semantic_coverage_audit.py`. It joins all semantic range catalogs across both
code islands and asserts zero unowned executable bytes while retaining five
embedded-data exclusions.

`cpu_entry_contract_catalog.csv` is generated by
`callable_contract_audit.py`. It joins all 61 externally meaningful CPU entry
kinds to complete semantic contracts and rejects any missing field or owner.

`ram_state_reference_catalog.csv` and `ram_state_semantics_catalog.csv` are
generated by `ram_state_reference_audit.py`. A linear decode of all 3,199
owned instructions proves `$3A-$3D/$43` unused, enumerates all 11 direct
`$082F` references, and records the resolved key-off/refresh/key-on/TL-bias bit
roles.

`external_question_catalog.csv` and `external_evidence_inventory.csv` are
generated by `external_evidence_audit.py`. They classify all 12 remaining
backlog questions by their required unavailable evidence and assert that no
statically actionable question remains.

`pitch_conversion_catalog.csv` is generated by `pitch_conversion_audit.py`.
It records overlapping POKEY/YM/table views, configured note-mode reachability,
corrections separating YM pitch registers from total-level processing, and the
configured absence of YM vibrato/frequency-envelope convergence.

`ym_pitch_validation_catalog.csv` is also generated by
`pitch_conversion_audit.py`. It combines ROM KC bytes for the stable command
`$04` voice with the supplied YMFM KC/KF composition, phase-step table,
prescale, and sample-rate equation. The corrected traversal excludes the
former command `$3A` pointer-table-as-note artifact.
Each row records its state assumptions; the implementation-derived clock is
kept at Strong-inference confidence.

`timing_clock_catalog.csv`, `timing_cycle_catalog.csv`,
`timing_duration_trace_catalog.csv`, `timing_loop_trace_catalog.csv`, and
`timing_articulation_trace_catalog.csv` are generated by
`timing_clock_audit.py`.
They record implementation-derived clocks and service cadences, ROM-proven
scheduler relationships, branch-qualified and instruction-executed 6502 cycle
counts, TMS5220 FIFO/watchdog checks, and selected carried-residue
duration/articulation traces after validating 61
instruction-byte anchors.

These files are generated from `soundrom.bin` and the persisted bounded
traversal catalogs by `rom_table_audit.py` and `cpu_traversal_audit.py`; they
should not be hand-edited.
